Privacy Policy

Aurora AI Technology Corp — SkillPlay Application

Last updated: May 2026

1. General Information

SkillPlay is a skill-based gaming platform operated by Aurora AI Technology Corp, a corporation registered in the state of Delaware, United States of America.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect the personal information of users of the SkillPlay mobile application and related services.

Contact: For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at legal@skillplay.app

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Username and email address upon registration
  • Identity data during KYC verification (full name, identity document, selfie photograph)
  • Banking information for withdrawals (IBAN, BIC/SWIFT, account holder name — we never store card data)
  • Communications with our support team

2.2 Automatically collected information

  • App usage data (games played, categories, results)
  • Device information (operating system, app version)
  • IP address and connection data
  • Transaction history within the platform

2.3 Third-party information

  • Identity verification results provided by Veriff
  • Payment processing data provided by Stripe

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Process payments, deposits, and fund withdrawals
  • Verify your identity as required by law (AMLD5, AML/KYC)
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and illegal activities
  • Send notifications related to your account and activity
  • Improve our services through anonymous usage analysis
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Address your support inquiries and requests

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We share information only in the following cases:

4.1 Service providers

  • Veriff — KYC identity verification
  • Stripe — Payment processing and transfers
  • Google Firebase — Database, authentication, and notifications
  • Anthropic — AI-powered question generation

4.2 Legal obligations

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or competent authority, particularly in matters of anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing.

5. Data Security

  • SSL/TLS encryption on all communications
  • Banking data never stored directly (Stripe tokenization)
  • Restricted access to personal data by staff
  • Continuous monitoring for unauthorized access
  • Enhanced authentication for financial operations (PSD2)

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: while account is active + 5 years
  • Financial transaction data: 7 years (legal obligation)
  • KYC data: 5 years from last transaction
  • Audit logs: 3 years

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@skillplay.app

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The SkillPlay mobile app does not use third-party tracking cookies. We only use essential technologies for app functionality, including Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage analysis.

9. Minors

SkillPlay is not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we detect that a user under 18 has created an account, we will delete it immediately.

10. International Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using SkillPlay from any country in the world, you agree that your personal data may be transferred, stored, and processed in these jurisdictions, in accordance with applicable data protection laws in each region. Aurora AI Technology Corp ensures that such transfers are carried out with appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where required by local legislation.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through an in-app notification or by email. Continued use of SkillPlay after changes are published implies acceptance of the new policy.
